# From Proven Innovation to a New Economic Vision

## Our Story: A Foundation of Proven Innovation

Most projects start with hype. Honeypot started with a problem.

In a landscape cluttered with yield farms and copy-paste forks, Honeypot’s team took a different path: building a modular, DAO-upgradeable decentralized exchange (DEX) from the ground up. The goal wasn’t to chase fleeting trends, but to create foundational infrastructure that solved real DeFi frictions — capital inefficiency, poor composability, and rigid liquidity systems.

That vision didn’t pay off as we are too far ahead of our industry. We pivoted our research efforts to another core DeFi pain point: combating sandwich attacks and MEV. In mid-2023, the protocol’s Batch A2MM model won the ETH Shanghai Hackathon, it wasn’t just a demo — it was proof that DeFi could be made smarter, leaner, fairer and more scalable.

From day one, Honeypot championed:

- Broad and useful DeFi innovation for our community

- Tools, not toys — simple, powerful interfaces for real financial use

This wasn’t about another DEX clone. It was a testbed for a broader thesis: if you solve composability and liquidity properly, everything else becomes possible.

# The Expansion: More Than a Product, An Entire Ecosystem

Pot2Pump, as revolutionary as it is, is just one part of a much broader strategy. Honeypot 2.0 is an expansion of our original vision into a comprehensive, all-in-one liquidity hub.

## 1. Cheese Benance: The Ecosystem Catalyst

One of the biggest challenges new Layer 1s face is the “cold start” problem. Our answer is Cheese Benance, a massive evolution of the “up-only” token concept. It’s a flexible factory for creating novel tokens backed by any asset (native L1 token or ERC20s), but it’s true power lies in its incentive loop: most fees are returned to the most active borrowers, directly rewarding participation and kickstarting a vibrant DeFi ecosystem.
