# Brand brief: Bros

## 1. Personality

Working hypothesis from the brief: crew, loyalty, play, straight talk; anti: casino, frat/degen, hype-bro. **Validated against the masterplan**:

| Hypothesis | Masterplan evidence | Verdict |
|---|---|---|
| crew | "launching a token means gathering people" `[02 §1]`; graduation needs 25 distinct people `[F05]`; squads `[F25, P1]` | keep |
| loyalty | holders paid for staying `[F10]`; creators locked by default `[F07]`; points reward holding, not churn `[08 §4.2]` | keep |
| play | seasons, badges, memes `[08 §4]` | keep, but no gambling framing `[10 §1.5]` |
| straight talk | "every rule readable before the first buy" `[02 §1]`; Fairness Card `[F06]` | keep: this is the strongest |
| anti casino | content policy bans "lottery", "jackpot", guaranteed returns `[10 §1]` | keep |
| anti degen/hype | no airdrop language, no platform token `[08 §1]` | keep |

**Three adjectives:** **Upfront** (rules first, numbers plain), **Together** (people are the unit, not wallets or volume), **Playful** (memes are fun; the interface can smile).
**Three anti-adjectives:** **Flashy** (no casino lights, no neon, no fake urgency), **Exclusive** (no clubhouse, no gatekeeping tone, no macho), **Pushy** (no FOMO, no "don't miss out", no countdown theatrics beyond the real window).

## 2. Voice and tone (English UI copy)

- **Say the rule, then the number.** "Everyone in this window pays the same price. Window closes in 01:12."
- **Short sentences, everyday words.** Grade-7 reading level for tooltips and errors.
- **Explain jargon once, where it appears**, with a tooltip: bonding curve, graduation, slippage, creator tax, Season Credit.
- **Never urge.** No exclamation marks in system copy. No "hurry", "last chance", "don't miss".
- **Never promise.** No "safe", "guaranteed", "profit", "moon", "100x". Graduation "is not an endorsement" `[10 §3]`.
- **The name carries the friendliness; the copy doesn't repeat it.** No "bro" jokes, no "ser/gm/wagmi", no slang in UI chrome. Marketing may use "your people" / "your squad" sparingly.
- **Tone shifts by moment:** neutral in trade flows, warm in onboarding and celebrations, flat and exact in errors and risks.
- Banned phrases: revolutionize, unlock, seamless, supercharge, to the moon, next-gen, plus Hallmark gate 19 list. No em/en dashes in UI copy (gate 19).

## 3. The name, and how to show it without the cliché

"Bros" says: *the people you go in with*. The masterplan's whole mechanic is that you don't win alone; you graduate a token together `[08 §4.1: "graduation becomes a social act"]`. So the name is expressed through **structure**, not imagery:

- **Pairs and groups as form.** The compact mark is a B built from one stem and two bowls: two members sharing one spine. No fists, handshakes, muscles, sunglasses, beer, frat letters or male figures.
- **People as the primary number.** "19 of 25 people" is set larger than price on cards. The brand is the count.
- **Same price for the window.** The brand promise is fairness among peers: nobody in the window gets a better price than their friends `[F02]`.
- **Inclusive by default.** The word is casual; the visuals and copy are gender-neutral. Illustrations (if any later) show mixed groups or no people at all; we never use AI faces or stock avatars (brief anti-slop charter). Avatars are generated identicons from addresses.

Things to avoid because of *other* Bros marks: red/white/yellow outlined arcade lettering and mushroom/plumber references (Super Mario Bros.); shield shapes and "WB" monograms (Warner Bros.); any "Bros." with a trailing period as a mark.
