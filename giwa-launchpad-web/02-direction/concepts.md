# Three concepts

Each concept differs in **macrostructure and genre**, not just colour. Hi-fi proofs: `concept-*/index.html` (landing first fold + token-page snippet); screenshots in `screens/`. All three use the same masterplan content so the comparison is fair: headline from GTM `[09 §0]`, 120 s window `[F02]`, 25 people `[F05]`, 1% fee split 40/30/30 `[08 §2]`. All numbers on the proofs are mock and badged.

---

## Concept 1: Block Grammar
- **Idea:** every screen is set like a Hangul syllable: square blocks built from a few strokes, placed by position.
- **Lenses:** Hangul as a designed system (initial/medial/final positions in a square), Korean fintech clarity, five-direction colour logic (calm neutrals, accents with fixed meanings).
- **Genre:** modern-minimal (custom).
- **Type:** Geologica (display, geometric, OFL) + Golos Text (UI, tabular figures, OFL).
- **Colour logic:** porcelain white + ink; yellow = "you / now"; red and blue reserved for price direction.
- **Grid:** square modules; cards are blocks with three fixed zones (picture, facts, state).
- **Signature motif:** the window block: deposits as squares in one square, clearing at one price; the B block mark.
- **Motion:** zones snap in order (picture → facts → state), 120 ms each.
- **Dense trading UI:** excellent; the modular grid is a natural pro-tool layout.
- **How it says Bros:** the B is a stem with two bowls; the people track is the second bar.
- **Risks:** reads as a tidy generic fintech; the grey square grid can be mistaken for a contribution heatmap.

## Concept 2: Signstack
- **Idea:** Discover reads like a Seoul street at noon: every live launch hangs its own sign, floor above floor; the lit number on each sign is people, not price.
- **Lenses:** Seoul signage and convenience-store graphics (density, stacked flat signs, condensed type, lit counters), webtoon vertical scroll for the landing story.
- **Genre:** playful (custom).
- **Type:** Antonio (condensed display, OFL) + Archivo (UI, tabular figures, OFL).
- **Colour logic:** daytime concrete + ink; yellow is the sign surface; red/blue appear only as sign panels in the proof (a flaw, see risks).
- **Grid:** vertical stack of full-width sign rows with stepped widths, like a facade; the shop floor below each sign is a calm two-column tool.
- **Signature motif:** the sign stack with lit people counters ("19/25").
- **Motion:** a sign switches on when its window opens; counters step, never roll; nothing else moves.
- **Dense trading UI:** good once the sign is confined to the header ("the shop floor stays calm").
- **How it says Bros:** a street of shops run by friends; every launch is somebody's shop on our street.
- **Risks:** red/blue sign panels collide with up/down semantics; five primaries edge toward an arcade palette (a famous "Bros" mark) and toward street-scene kitsch; colour panels with light text strain contrast at small sizes.

## Concept 3: Bojagi
- **Idea:** a launch is a patchwork: every person's piece is stitched into one cloth.
- **Lenses:** bojagi patchwork (uneven modules joined by seams), hanji/moon-jar restraint, natural-dye five-direction colours (indigo, safflower, ochre).
- **Genre:** editorial (custom).
- **Type:** Castoro (roman serif display, OFL) + Onest (UI, OFL).
- **Colour logic:** ramie green-grey cloth + indigo-black ink; ochre = you; safflower/indigo for direction.
- **Grid:** quilt of uneven patches with 2 px seams; treemaps for the window and holders.
- **Signature motif:** the window quilt: deposits as patches sized by amount, stitched to one price.
- **Motion:** patches stitch in along seams; slow and calm.
- **Dense trading UI:** fair; seams compete with table rules; serif numbers are elegant but slower to read.
- **How it says Bros:** many pieces, one cloth.
- **Risks:** quiet and heritage-leaning for a meme product (Playful scores low); serif on light paper sits near a known AI look; ochre accent fails 4.5:1 for small text.
