## Why this one (evidence)

- **Distinctiveness:** at thumbnail size the sign stack is unlike every launchpad grid we know (competitors use uniform card grids on dark) `[RESEARCH 35–38]`.
- **Personality:** "the lit number is people, not price" is the masterplan's card rule turned into the brand: people count on the card, no volume on the default card `[06 §2.1]`.
- **Korean, by principle:** stacked flat signs and condensed lettering from Seoul street signage `[RESEARCH 23, 25]`, used as a system, with zero Hangul.
- **Dense data:** once confined, signs cost one row per token; the token page is a standard chart + panel layout users expect `[RESEARCH S6]`.

## What we decided against
- **Dark default:** light daytime street is the reference design; dark ("the street at night") is parity, following `prefers-color-scheme` `[Q16]`.
- **Neon:** night mode signs are flat plates with no glow (anti-slop charter).
- **Caps everywhere:** names on signs and heads are set in caps by CSS; labels and body stay sentence case (Hallmark 67).

## Signature, one per surface
- **Landing:** the 3D "level" moment: a block of deposits of different heights settles into one flat level when the example window closes (see `05-motion-3d-video/3d-spec.md`).
- **Discover:** the sign stack.
- **Token:** the sign header with its people counter; in the Window state, the window quilt.
- **Share card:** the sign, alone.

The masterplan's earlier direction (celadon/persimmon, Pretendard, "Madang ring") is superseded; logged as `_open-questions.md` Q10. The dual-track ring survives as the **two-track meter** (credit and people), drawn as bars on the shop floor.
