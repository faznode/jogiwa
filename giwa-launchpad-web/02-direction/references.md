## 1. Studied references (Hallmark `study` substitute)

Hallmark v2.0.0 has no `study` verb (see playbook). For each of these six we captured one viewport at 1280×800 and extracted DNA in Hallmark's vocabulary: macrostructure, type pairing, colour anchor. Then: where we deliberately diverge.

| # | Reference | Macrostructure | Type pairing | Colour anchor | What we learn | Where Bros diverges (and must not copy) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| S1 | **Toss** — https://toss.im | Poster: one full-bleed photo, one statement, minimal nav | proprietary geometric sans, very large, white on photo | white + one brand blue mark | Korean fintech plain-language clarity: one sentence per screen, one task per screen | no photography of people, no rounded hero card, no Toss blue or Toss Product Sans; our hero is the product mechanic, not a lifestyle |
| S2 | **Linear** — https://linear.app | Stage: headline left, product UI below the fold line | neo-grotesk display + same family UI | near-black, one white CTA | the product itself as the picture; density without noise | no dark-by-default, no gradient-lit product screenshot; light concrete ground |
| S3 | **Teenage Engineering** — https://teenage.engineering | Object hero, idiosyncratic pictogram nav | small grotesk labels, lots of air | black, a single orange detail | one object carries the brand; nav can have character | no pictogram nav (theirs), no product spotlight; our "object" is the sign, made of type |
| S4 | **WEBTOON** — https://www.webtoons.com/en/ | Ranked rows of covers, big ordinal numerals overlapping covers | rounded sans, bold numerals | white + brand green | ranking numerals are honest structure when the list *is* a ranking | numerals only on the leaderboard (a real sequence), never decorative; no cover-card layout |
| S5 | **The Pudding** — https://pudding.cool | Index of issues on solid colour blocks | condensed serif heads + plain sans | magenta/purple/yellow blocks | solid flat colour fields carry personality; data storytelling | not their logo style or block colours; we use ink/yellow plates only, and colour never fights data semantics |
| S6 | **Upbit** (exchange view) — https://upbit.com/exchange | Pro tool: chart centre, order book, order form right, coin list far right | system Korean sans, tabular numbers | blue header; **red up / blue down**; buy tab red, sell tab blue | Korean price convention and buy/sell colour mapping; trade-panel placement users already know | not Upbit's blue header, column layout or trade dress; we keep only the convention (chart + panel right) and the colour mapping as a user setting |
