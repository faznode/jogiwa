## Construction
- **Letters:** "ros" are Antonio Bold outlines (OFL permits use in a logo); the **B is drawn**: one stem, two bowls, and a hairline gap between the bowls. Two people sharing one spine: the brand idea, visible only when you look.
- **Proportions (font units, cap = 1760):** stem 268, bars 198, mid bars 123, bowl gap 54, upper bowl 2.78 × stem wide, lower 2.98 × stem (the lower bowl is wider, as in a well-made B).
- **Plate:** padding 0.34 × cap left/right, 0.30 × cap top/bottom; corner radius 0.06 × cap. The plate is part of the mark: it is a sign.
- **Tracking:** −40 units between letters.
- **Mark:** B at 0.60 × the square, optically centred; plate radius 0.12 × size.

## Clear space and sizes
- Clear space on all sides = the height of the plate's top padding (0.30 × cap). Nothing enters it, including the testnet strip.
- Minimum sizes: wordmark plate 24 px tall on screen (the hairline gap disappears below 64 px; that is fine); mark 16 px (favicon).
- Never: recolour outside ink/yellow/paper; stretch; outline; add a trailing period; set "BROS" in caps; place on a red or blue field; add shadows or glow.

## Name rule
Always **Bros**: capital B, no trailing period, never "Bros." and never "BROS" (the plate wordmark is the styled form; it is mixed case).

## Collision check (other "Bros" marks)
- No red/white outlined arcade lettering, no rounded bubbly face, no multicolour letters (a famous game series).
- No shield, no WB-style monogram (a famous film studio).
- Our mark is a condensed grotesk on a flat sign plate in ink and yellow: different shape, different colour, different construction.
