# Glossary (tooltip copy, plain language)

Mirrors `strings/en.json` → `glossary.*`. Each definition is the tooltip text; sources in brackets for the facts.

| Term | Tooltip |
|---|---|
| Opening window | The first two minutes of every launch. Everyone who joins pays the same price when it closes, so being fastest doesn't help. `[F02]` |
| Humans-first | For the first minutes, only verified wallets can join, with a cap per person. `[F03]` |
| Verified (tier 1, tier 2) | Your wallet holds a Dojang attestation. Tier 1 comes from the GIWA faucet, tier 2 from an Upbit-verified address. `[F04]` |
| Bonding curve | Before graduation, the price follows a fixed formula: it rises as people buy and falls as they sell. `[F01]` |
| People threshold | A token needs 25 different verified people holding it, plus enough credit, to graduate. `[F05]` |
| Graduation | When a token meets both goals it moves to a pool that can never be withdrawn. Graduation is not an endorsement. `[F05, F08, 10 §3]` |
| Fairness Card | Six facts about a launch, read from the chain: creator share and lock, creator tax, window result, people needed, where fees go, team wallets. `[F06]` |
| Creator tax | An optional extra fee of 0 to 3% that goes to the creator on every trade. It's printed on the Fairness Card. `[08 §2]` |
| Trade fee | 1% of each trade. 40% goes to the creator, 30% to holders, 30% to the protocol. `[08 §2]` |
| Slippage | How much worse than the quote you'll accept if the price moves before your trade lands. If it moves more, nothing is spent. |
| Price impact | How much your own trade moves the price. Big trades in small pools move it more. |
| Season Credit (SC) | Practice currency for this testnet season. You get a daily amount by tier. It can't be sent to others and has no monetary value. `[08 §4.1]` |
| Holder rewards | A share of trade fees paid daily to people holding the token. `[F10]` |
| Vesting | The creator's tokens stay locked until graduation, then unlock bit by bit over 30 days. `[F07]` |
| Keeper | Anyone who presses the button that settles a window or graduates a token. They earn a small bounty. `[F13]` |
| Clearing price | The single price everyone in the window pays, known when the window closes. `[F02]` |
| Team wallet | A wallet the creator declared as theirs. Its trades are labelled so you can see them. `[F15]` |
